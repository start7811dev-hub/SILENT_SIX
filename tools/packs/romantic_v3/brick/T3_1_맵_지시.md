## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

You are painting one game-map background for a real-time stealth tactics game. `ref/look_outdoor.jpg` is a finished map from the same game. **Copy its look exactly**: canvas, camera angle, painterly finish, level of detail, warm practical lights against cool ambience, black surround. **Do not copy its layout, its season or its decorations.** The layout below is a level design and every placement in it matters for gameplay.

Make **one image**. Output file name: `bgp_brick_3.jpg`. Do not ask questions.

### Canvas, camera, scale
- Exactly **1536x1024**, landscape. One plain diamond-shaped plan filling the frame, 2:1 isometric, same camera as the reference. Outside the diamond: flat near-black `#06080b`.
- The plan is **28 x 28 metres**: a person would be about 32 px tall.
- Pre-dawn. Grey stone, dust haze, sodium work lamps, a red warning beacon.

### Words used below
Corners of the diamond: **TOP** (far), **BOTTOM** (near), **LEFT**, **RIGHT**.
Perimeter sides: **upper-left side** (TOP to LEFT), **upper-right side** (TOP to RIGHT), **lower-left side** (LEFT to BOTTOM), **lower-right side** (RIGHT to BOTTOM).
"Parallel to a side" means running along that side's isometric axis.

### Rules that apply to every map in this set
1. Everything solid is straight and runs along the two isometric axes. No diagonal walls, no curves unless the layout names one.
2. **The two near sides (lower-left and lower-right) are closed by a thin, low boundary only about 1 m high** (a low fence or kerb), so that the floor just inside them stays fully visible. Only the two far sides may be tall.
3. **A clear walkable lane at least 2 m wide runs along the inside of all four sides**, unless the layout says this rule does not apply.
4. Everything is **single-storey and low**, so the lanes behind it stay visible. Rooms and buildings that can be entered have the **roof removed and walls cut at one height with a flat dark top**. Every doorway named in the layout is **visibly open, 2 m wide**.
5. Walkable floor is calm, even and clearly readable, slightly brighter than the structures on it. Water, drops and voids clearly read as "cannot walk here" unless the layout says they are walkable.
6. **No loose objects anywhere**: no furniture, crates, barrels, vehicles, carts, plants in pots, signs, barriers, people or animals. They are added later as separate sprites. Only what the layout calls fixed or built-in is painted. Lamps fixed to walls or posts are fine.
7. No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, grid lines, arrows, labels or watermark.

### The place
A working quarry cut into three terraces that step up from the near side to the far side.

### Layout
**Three terraces.** The floor is cut into **three flat terraces, each a strip about 8 m deep running the full width of the map parallel to the lower-left side**. The **lower terrace** touches the lower-left side, the **middle terrace** is 2 m higher, the **upper terrace** is 2 m higher again and touches the upper-right side. Between terraces is a **vertical cut rock face 2 m high**: solid.

**Ramps (the only ways up).** Lower to middle: **two rock ramps, each 3 m wide**, one near the upper-left side and one in the centre. Middle to upper: **two ramps**, one near the lower-right side and one a third of the way from the upper-left side. The ramps are not lined up.

**Lower terrace.** From the upper-left end: two **site cabins**, each 3 x 6 m, roof removed, one doorway each, with a 2 m gap between them; then a flat steel **weighbridge plate**, 3 x 8 m, set flush in the ground.

**Middle terrace.** A fixed **stone crusher**, 5 x 8 m, tall and solid, with one conveyor arm resting on the upper rock face. Further along, **three concrete-walled stockpile bays**, each 4 x 4 m, open on the near side, walls 2 m high, with a heap of crushed stone inside each.

**Upper terrace.** Toward the RIGHT corner: the **explosives magazine**, a concrete bunker 4 x 5 m, roof on, steel door facing the terrace, surrounded on three sides by an earth bank. Toward the TOP corner: the **blast bench**, a flat area 8 x 6 m marked off by red-and-white stakes, its floor dotted with a neat grid of drill holes.

**Edges.** The two far sides are the high quarry wall. **Way in:** a gate in the low fence on the lower-left side near the BOTTOM corner.

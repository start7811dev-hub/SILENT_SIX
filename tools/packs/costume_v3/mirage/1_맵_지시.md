## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

You are painting one game-map background for a real-time stealth tactics game. `ref/look_indoor.jpg` is a finished map from the same game. **Copy its look exactly**: canvas, camera angle, painterly finish, level of detail, warm practical lights against cool ambience, black surround. **Do not copy its layout, its season or its decorations.** The layout below is a level design and every placement in it matters for gameplay.

Make **one image**. Output file name: `bgc_mirage.jpg`. Do not ask questions.

### Canvas, camera, scale
- Exactly **1536x1024**, landscape. One plain diamond-shaped plan filling the frame, 2:1 isometric, same camera as the reference. Outside the diamond: flat near-black `#06080b`.
- The plan is **32 x 32 metres**: a person would be about 28 px tall.
- A private casino at night. Gold lamps over green baize, black marble, a skyline of city lights beyond the terrace, cold white light in the back rooms.

### Words used below
Corners of the diamond: **TOP** (far), **BOTTOM** (near), **LEFT**, **RIGHT**.
Perimeter sides: **upper-left side** (TOP to LEFT), **upper-right side** (TOP to RIGHT), **lower-left side** (LEFT to BOTTOM), **lower-right side** (RIGHT to BOTTOM).
"Parallel to a side" means running along that side's isometric axis.

### Rules that apply to every map in this set
1. Everything solid is straight and runs along the two isometric axes. No diagonal walls, no curves unless the layout names one.
2. **The two near sides (lower-left and lower-right) are closed by a thin, low boundary only about 1 m high** (the wall cut down to a 1 m stub), so that the floor just inside them stays fully visible. Only the two far sides may be tall.
3. **A clear walkable lane at least 2 m wide runs along the inside of all four sides**, unless the layout says this rule does not apply.
4. Everything is **single-storey and low**, so the lanes behind it stay visible. Rooms and buildings that can be entered have the **roof removed and walls cut at one height with a flat dark top**. Every doorway named in the layout is **visibly open, 2 m wide**.
5. Walkable floor is calm, even and clearly readable, slightly brighter than the structures on it. Water, drops and voids clearly read as "cannot walk here" unless the layout says they are walkable.
6. **No loose objects anywhere**: no furniture, crates, barrels, vehicles, carts, plants in pots, signs, barriers, people or animals. They are added later as separate sprites. Only what the layout calls fixed or built-in is painted. Lamps fixed to walls or posts are fine.
7. No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, grid lines, arrows, labels or watermark.

### The place
The interior of a penthouse casino: a gaming floor with a cashier cage in its centre, a bar, a VIP salon, a terrace, and secure rooms behind the far walls.

### Layout
**Gaming floor.** One large room, 20 x 20 m, toward the near half of the map, with a deep green-and-gold carpet. **The floor is empty** (tables are sprites).

**Cage.** In the **exact centre of the gaming floor**: the cashier cage, 5 x 5 m, brass bars on a marble base, one barred gate on its far side. Solid walls of bars; you can see in.

**Slot machines.** **Along both far walls of the gaming floor** (the upper-left wall and the upper-right wall): one continuous built-in row of slot machines against each wall, glowing, each row broken once by a doorway.

**Bar.** Along the gaming floor's lower-left wall: a fixed bar counter 1 x 9 m with a mirrored back shelf, and a 2 m space behind it for the staff.

**Terrace.** Along the lower-right side: an open terrace 3 m deep, full length, behind a glass wall with two doorways, a low glass balustrade and the city far below.

**VIP salon.** At the LEFT corner: a room 6 x 7 m, dark red carpet, one doorway from the gaming floor screened by a fixed carved partition.

**Back corridor.** Behind the two far walls of the gaming floor, an L-shaped staff corridor 2 m wide with a plain grey floor, reached through the doorways in the slot rows.

**Rooms off the back corridor:** the **surveillance room**, 5 x 5 m, along the upper-left side, with a built-in wall of monitors; the **count room**, 5 x 6 m, at the TOP corner, with a steel door and a built-in counting table; a **staff changing room**, 4 x 5 m, along the upper-right side.

**Way in:** the lift lobby in the near wall at the BOTTOM corner, with a floor hazard stripe.

**Floors tell the zones apart.** The public, show-side floors and the staff-only back rooms must have clearly different floor materials and colours, as described, so a player can tell at a glance which zone they are standing in.

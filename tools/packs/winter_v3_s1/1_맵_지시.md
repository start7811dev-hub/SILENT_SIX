## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

You are painting one game-map background for a real-time stealth tactics game. `ref/look_market.jpg` is the current version of this map. **Copy its look exactly**: canvas, camera, scale, materials, lighting, level of detail, black surround. **Do not copy its layout.** The layout below is a new level design and every placement in it matters for gameplay.

Make **one image**. Output file name: `bgw1_v3.jpg`. Do not ask questions.

### Canvas and camera
- Exactly **1536x1024**, landscape. One plain diamond-shaped plan filling the frame like the reference, 2:1 isometric, same camera angle. Background outside the diamond: flat near-black `#06080b`.
- The plan is a walled old-town square, **20 x 20 metres**. A person would be about 45 px tall. A market stall is about 3 m long and 1.5 m deep.
- Night, falling snow already settled on roofs, warm string lights and lamps, cool blue ambient light.

### Words used below
The diamond has four corners: **TOP** (far), **BOTTOM** (near), **LEFT**, **RIGHT**.
It has four perimeter walls: **upper-left wall** (TOP to LEFT), **upper-right wall** (TOP to RIGHT), **lower-left wall** (LEFT to BOTTOM), **lower-right wall** (RIGHT to BOTTOM).
"Parallel to a wall" means running along that wall's isometric axis.

### Structure rules
1. Everything solid is straight and runs along the two isometric axes. No diagonal walls. The only round things allowed are the tree, the carousel and the fountain bowl, and each of them sits inside a square footprint.
2. Perimeter: low arcaded town buildings on all four sides, like the reference, cut at one height.
3. **Four gates, one in the exact middle of each perimeter wall**, identical stone arches, 2 m wide, all visibly open with paving running through. No other openings.
4. Stalls are **tall roofed wooden booths that block sight**: closed back and sides, open counter on one long side, snow on striped awnings. Gaps between stalls in a row are 1.5 to 2 m wide and clearly open.
5. Walkable ground is calm, even cobblestone with thin snow, slightly brighter than the structures. Lanes at least 2 m wide unless stated.

### Layout (nine parts)

**A. Entry court.** The gate in the middle of the **lower-left wall** is the way in. Paint a floor hazard stripe across its threshold. Just inside it is a quiet, empty court about 5 x 4 m.

**B. Screen row.** 4 m inside the lower-left wall, a single straight row of **four stalls parallel to the lower-left wall**, backs toward the entry court, counters toward the centre. The row spans most of the width and has **exactly three gaps**: one near its left end, one in the middle, one near its right end. This row hides the entry court from the rest of the square.

**C. The tree.** Exact centre of the square: a large decorated Christmas tree inside a **square wrought-iron fence, 5 x 5 m**, chest-high. The fence has **one gate only, in the middle of the side that faces the upper-right wall**. The gate is closed. Under the tree, just inside that gate, one bright red gift box with a gold ribbon: the most eye-catching object on the map.

**D. Stall quarter.** Between the tree fence and the **upper-left wall**: **two rows of three stalls each, both rows parallel to the upper-left wall**. The gaps of one row are offset from the gaps of the other, so you cannot see straight across. The lane between the two rows is 2 m wide. The lane between the inner row and the tree fence is 2 m wide.

**E. Service alley.** Between the outer stall row and the upper-left wall: a **narrow back alley, about 1.5 m wide**, darker than the lanes, running the full length of the stall quarter. The gate in the middle of the **upper-left wall** opens into this alley.

**F. Stage.** Between the tree fence and the **lower-right wall**: a raised wooden stage with a roofed back wall and red curtains, **6 m long and 2 m deep, long side parallel to the lower-right wall**, open front facing the tree. The stage is solid: nobody walks through it.

**G. Audience plaza.** Between the stage front and the tree fence: an **open, completely empty paved area about 4 m wide and 8 m long**, the brightest floor on the map, lit by stage spotlights. Nothing stands on it.

**H. Backstage corridor and generator.** Between the back of the stage and the lower-right wall: a **2 m wide corridor**, dim. The gate in the middle of the **lower-right wall** opens into this corridor. At the end of the corridor toward the **RIGHT** corner: a diesel generator in a small chain-link cage against the wall, with a thick cable running along the ground to the stage. The cage door is open.

**I. Far side.** The gate in the middle of the **upper-right wall** faces a straight, clear promenade about 5 m wide that runs to the tree-fence gate.
- Between the promenade and the **TOP** corner: a small carousel inside a **square fence about 5 x 5 m**. It blocks sight.
- Between the promenade and the **RIGHT** corner: a **square frozen fountain, about 4 x 4 m**, with a waist-high stone rim and solid ice inside. People cannot walk on it.

## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

`ref/style_cast.jpg` shows the cast of the game SILENT SIX. Match its art style: semi-realistic anime illustration, painterly high-end finish, dramatic two-colour lighting. **Do not draw any of those six women.** You are designing a new character.

Make the images **one per reply**, in the order below. Write the output file name on its own line before the image. After each image stop and wait for "next". Do not ask questions.

### The character — code name SANTA
- An adult woman in her **late thirties**, former field leader of a black-ops unit. Tall, long-legged, strong shoulders, narrow waist: an hourglass outline that reads clearly even as a pure silhouette. She stands like someone who has never been in a hurry.
- **Outfit, identical in every image**: a long, tailored **crimson wool coat** to mid-calf, belted tight at the waist, with a **deep hood** and wide **white fur trim** on the hood, cuffs and hem. Black leather gloves. Black knee-high boots with a slim high heel. A thin gold chain belt-charm shaped like a small bell at her hip.
- **Hair**: very long, loose, heavy waves down to the small of her back, deep auburn. It spills forward out of the hood on one side.
- **Face** (hidden for most of the set): dark red lips, a small beauty mark under the left corner of the mouth, amber eyes, a calm, knowing half-smile.
- Signature colours: crimson `#C8102E`, fur white `#F2EEE6`, gold `#D8B25A`, shadow `#0A0C10`.
- She is fully clothed in every image. Her appeal comes from outline, posture and the half-hidden face, never from exposed skin. No weapons.

### Image 1 — `santa_ref.png` — reference sheet
1536x1024, plain dark charcoal background. Three full-body views of SANTA side by side, evenly lit enough to read the costume: front view, three-quarter view, back view. **Hood up in all three, face in deep shadow below the nose** so only lips and chin show. At the right edge, four flat colour swatches of the signature colours. No text.
Use this sheet as the single source of truth for the six images that follow.

### Rules for images 2 to 6 — dialogue portraits
- Vertical 2:3, **1024x1536**, **transparent background** (PNG). If you cannot output transparency, use flat pure green `#00FF00` with no shadow on it.
- One figure, framed from mid-thigh up, large in the frame. Her body is turned so that she **faces RIGHT** (toward the person she is talking to).
- Light comes from behind her: a hard crimson rim light along one edge and a thin cool-white rim on the other. The front of the figure is as dark as each image says.
- No background objects, no snow particles outside her outline, no text.

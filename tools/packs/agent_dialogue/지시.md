# SILENT SIX — 요원 대화용 일러스트 (6명 × 표정 3종 = 18장)

## 사용법 (사람이 읽는 부분)

1. ChatGPT 새 대화에 이 zip을 올리고 **"압축을 풀고 지시.md 를 읽은 뒤 RAVEN 부터 한 장씩 진행해 줘."** 라고 보낸다.
2. 한 번에 한 장만 나온다. 다음 장은 "next". 한 요원(3장)이 끝나면 새 대화로 넘어가는 편이 얼굴이 덜 흐트러진다.
3. 지금 급한 것은 **RAVEN 3장**이다(블러디 크리스마스 1판). 나머지는 2~5판과 코스튬 미션에서 쓴다.
4. 파일명 그대로 Claude에게 넘긴다. 배경을 지워서 게임에 넣는다.

검수: 몸이 **왼쪽**을 보고 있는지, 무기를 들고 있지 않은지, 허벅지 중간 위로 잘렸는지, 세 장의 얼굴과 복장이 같은지, 배경이 한 가지 색인지.

## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

Read `agents/AGENTS.md` first. It defines the six women of SILENT SIX and how to read each `agents/sheet_<name>.jpg`. Panel `NAME-A` is the face, panel `NAME-C` is the tactical outfit. `ref/format.png` shows the format these portraits must follow (framing, size of the figure, flat background). It is a different outfit; copy the format, not the clothes.

These are visual-novel conversation portraits. In the game the agent stands on the RIGHT of the screen and talks to someone on the LEFT.

Make **one image per reply**. Write the output file name on its own line before the image. After each image, stop and wait for "next". Do not ask questions.

### Rules for every portrait
1. Vertical 2:3, **1024x1536**. **Transparent background** (PNG). If you cannot output transparency, use flat pure green `#00FF00` everywhere behind her, with no shadow and no gradient.
2. One woman, framed from **mid-thigh up**, large in the frame, standing. Her body and face are turned so that she **faces LEFT** (three-quarter view toward the left edge).
3. **Tactical outfit exactly as in her `NAME-C` panel**, face and hair exactly as in `NAME-A`. Her signature colour visible. The three portraits of one agent must be the same woman in the same outfit: only pose and expression change.
4. **No weapons in hand, nothing aimed.** Holstered gear on the body is fine. Hands are free: crossed arms, hand on hip, hand to earpiece, and so on.
5. Even, readable light from the front-left with a thin rim light in her signature colour. No background objects, no floor, no text, no effects outside her outline.
6. Art style of the sheets: semi-realistic anime illustration, painterly high-end finish. Adult woman. Fully dressed in her mission gear.

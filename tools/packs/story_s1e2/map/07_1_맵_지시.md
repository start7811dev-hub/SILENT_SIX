## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

You are painting one game-map background for a real-time stealth tactics game (story mission 07, "빈 책상"). Abandoned underground records level of a government building. A clean-up squad is burning files. Make **one image**. Output file name: `bg_s1_07.jpg`. Do not ask questions.

### Canvas and camera
- Exactly **1536x1024**, landscape. One diamond-shaped plan filling the frame, 2:1 isometric, the same camera angle and painterly finish as `ref/look_indoor_ep1.jpg`. Outside the diamond: flat near-black `#06080b`.
- Scale like `ref/look_scale_30m.jpg`: a person would be about 30 px tall. Copy the **look** of the references, never their layout.
- Night. Fluorescent strip lights mostly dead; cold blue emergency lighting in corridors, warm orange glow and smoke haze from the burning room at RIGHT.

### Words used below
Corners of the diamond: **TOP** (far), **BOTTOM** (near), **LEFT**, **RIGHT**. Perimeter sides: **upper-left** (TOP–LEFT), **upper-right** (TOP–RIGHT), **lower-left** (LEFT–BOTTOM), **lower-right** (RIGHT–BOTTOM). The lower-left and lower-right perimeter walls are cut low so the inside stays visible.

### Grid-aligned map rules (very important: the game collides on a 1-metre grid laid over your painting)
- The plan is exactly 30 x 30 metres. Every wall, fence, kerb, counter and furniture edge lies on a whole-metre line. 
- Walls are solid bands exactly 1 metre thick, cut off at waist height (about 1.2 m) so every floor tile stays visible. No roofs, ceilings, upper floors or overhangs.
- Only straight walls along the two isometric axes. No diagonal or curved walls; round things sit inside square footprints.
- Every doorway and corridor is exactly 2 metres wide; main corridors 3 metres. Doors are drawn open (an empty gap), never half-open.
- Fixed furniture and machinery have whole-metre footprints (1x1, 1x2, 2x2, 2x3 m) and are clearly darker or taller than the floor.
- Walkable floor is one calm, even, clearly readable material per zone. No floor-coloured low obstacles, no clutter, no loose objects.

### The plan
- **Entry, lower-left side**: a 3 m wide car-park ramp coming down through the lower-left wall into a small **guard post** (4 x 4 m room with a counter) at BOTTOM-LEFT. Paint a floor hazard stripe across the ramp threshold.
- **Main corridor ring**: a 3 m wide corridor runs in a full rectangle around the centre, about 4 m inside the perimeter. Every room opens onto it through 2 m doorways.
- **Centre block (10 x 10 m)**: the **archive stacks** — long parallel shelving rows 1 m deep with 2 m aisles between them, running from upper-left to lower-right. Five rows. Shelves are taller than a person and block sight.
- **TOP corner room (7 x 7 m): the commander's office.** One large empty desk at the centre facing the door, a dark computer terminal on it, a single chair pushed back, a wall of empty frames. One 2 m door onto the corridor. This is the goal: make the desk the most clearly lit object on the map (one desk lamp).
- **LEFT corner room (6 x 6 m): the server room.** Two rows of server racks (1 x 3 m each) with a 2 m aisle, blinking teal lights, one breaker panel on the far wall.
- **RIGHT corner room (7 x 7 m): the burn room.** Two steel drum incinerators (2 x 2 m footprints), scattered half-burnt paper on the floor drawn flat, orange firelight and smoke. Its door to the corridor is open.
- **Upper-left side, middle: the CCTV control room** (5 x 4 m): a desk with a wall of dark monitors.
- **Upper-right side, middle: the emergency stairwell** (4 x 4 m), a 2 m opening in the upper-right wall with a floor hazard stripe: the second exit.
- **Lower-right side, middle**: a filing room (5 x 4 m) with two rows of low cabinets (1 x 2 m), open door.
- Between the rooms and the perimeter: solid 1 m walls only. The corridor ring is the only way around.

### Do not paint
- No loose objects on the floor: no crates, barrels, chairs, carts, vehicles, boxes, bins, trolleys, plants in pots, signboards. They are added later as separate sprites.
- No characters, people, animals, text, numbers, logos, UI, grid lines, arrows, labels or watermark.

### Before you finish, check
Whole-metre alignment, 1 m wall bands, 2 m doorways, no roofs or upper floors, clean floor, the entry and the second exit both marked with a hazard stripe, and the goal object clearly lit.

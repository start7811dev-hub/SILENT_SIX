## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

You are painting one game-map background for a real-time stealth tactics game (story mission 12, "일곱 번째 의자"). The witness wing of a parliament building on the morning of a hearing. Make **one image**. Output file name: `bg_s1_12.jpg`. Do not ask questions.

### Canvas and camera
- Exactly **1536x1024**, landscape. One diamond-shaped plan filling the frame, 2:1 isometric, the same camera angle and painterly finish as `ref/look_indoor_ep1.jpg`. Outside the diamond: flat near-black `#06080b`.
- Scale like `ref/look_scale_30m.jpg`: a person would be about 30 px tall. Copy the **look** of the references, never their layout.
- Night. Early morning before a hearing. Polished marble, warm brass lamps, pale dawn through tall windows on the upper walls, a lit press room.

### Words used below
Corners of the diamond: **TOP** (far), **BOTTOM** (near), **LEFT**, **RIGHT**. Perimeter sides: **upper-left** (TOP–LEFT), **upper-right** (TOP–RIGHT), **lower-left** (LEFT–BOTTOM), **lower-right** (RIGHT–BOTTOM). The lower-left and lower-right perimeter walls are cut low so the inside stays visible.

### Grid-aligned map rules (very important: the game collides on a 1-metre grid laid over your painting)
- The plan is exactly 30 x 30 metres. Every wall, fence, kerb, counter and furniture edge lies on a whole-metre line. 
- Walls are solid bands exactly 1 metre thick, cut off at waist height (about 1.2 m) so every floor tile stays visible. No roofs, ceilings, upper floors or overhangs.
- Only straight walls along the two isometric axes. No diagonal or curved walls; round things sit inside square footprints.
- Every doorway and corridor is exactly 2 metres wide; main corridors 3 metres. Doors are drawn open (an empty gap), never half-open.
- Fixed furniture and machinery have whole-metre footprints (1x1, 1x2, 2x2, 2x3 m) and are clearly darker or taller than the floor.
- Walkable floor is one calm, even, clearly readable material per zone. No floor-coloured low obstacles, no clutter, no loose objects.

### The plan
- **Grand marble lobby (10 x 8 m)** at BOTTOM, entered through a 3 m doorway in the lower-left wall (hazard stripe). Two square columns (2 x 2 m) and a reception counter (1 x 4 m).
- **Witness corridor**: a 3 m corridor runs from the lobby up the centre toward TOP. On both sides of it, **six witness waiting rooms**, each 5 x 4 m with one 2 m door onto the corridor: a sofa, a low table, a water jug on it drawn flat. Rooms alternate left and right.
- **TOP corner: the hearing room antechamber (7 x 6 m)** with a tall double door drawn shut on the far wall (the hearing room is off-map) and a long table where an aide's briefcase lies (the goal), under a brass lamp.
- **LEFT corner: security office (6 x 5 m)**: desks, a radio rack, a key cabinet.
- **RIGHT corner: press briefing room (7 x 6 m)**: a podium, rows of chairs drawn as fixed blocks (1 x 4 m rows), a camera platform (2 x 2 m).
- **Lower-right side: the service stair down to the underground link tunnel** (3 x 3 m opening with a hazard stripe) — the exit after the alarm.
- A 2 m service passage runs behind the waiting rooms on each side, linking LEFT and RIGHT rooms without the main corridor.

### Do not paint
- No loose objects on the floor: no crates, barrels, chairs, carts, vehicles, boxes, bins, trolleys, plants in pots, signboards. They are added later as separate sprites.
- No characters, people, animals, text, numbers, logos, UI, grid lines, arrows, labels or watermark.

### Before you finish, check
Whole-metre alignment, 1 m wall bands, 2 m doorways, no roofs or upper floors, clean floor, the entry and the second exit both marked with a hazard stripe, and the goal object clearly lit.

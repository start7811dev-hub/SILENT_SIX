## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

You are painting one game-map background for a real-time stealth tactics game (story mission 11, "철수 명령서"). A waste incineration plant used to destroy secret documents. Make **one image**. Output file name: `bg_s1_11.jpg`. Do not ask questions.

### Canvas and camera
- Exactly **1536x1024**, landscape. One diamond-shaped plan filling the frame, 2:1 isometric, the same camera angle and painterly finish as `ref/look_outdoor_ep1.jpg`. Outside the diamond: flat near-black `#06080b`.
- Scale like `ref/look_scale_30m.jpg`: a person would be about 30 px tall. Copy the **look** of the references, never their layout.
- Night. Industrial night. The furnace mouth glows orange at the centre; floodlights on poles; drifting ash; wet concrete.

### Words used below
Corners of the diamond: **TOP** (far), **BOTTOM** (near), **LEFT**, **RIGHT**. Perimeter sides: **upper-left** (TOP–LEFT), **upper-right** (TOP–RIGHT), **lower-left** (LEFT–BOTTOM), **lower-right** (RIGHT–BOTTOM). The lower-left and lower-right perimeter walls are cut low so the inside stays visible.

### Grid-aligned map rules (very important: the game collides on a 1-metre grid laid over your painting)
- The plan is exactly 30 x 30 metres. Every wall, fence, kerb, counter and furniture edge lies on a whole-metre line. 
- Walls are solid bands exactly 1 metre thick, cut off at waist height (about 1.2 m) so every floor tile stays visible. No roofs, ceilings, upper floors or overhangs.
- Only straight walls along the two isometric axes. No diagonal or curved walls; round things sit inside square footprints.
- Every doorway and corridor is exactly 2 metres wide; main corridors 3 metres. Doors are drawn open (an empty gap), never half-open.
- Fixed furniture and machinery have whole-metre footprints (1x1, 1x2, 2x2, 2x3 m) and are clearly darker or taller than the floor.
- Walkable floor is one calm, even, clearly readable material per zone. No floor-coloured low obstacles, no clutter, no loose objects.

### The plan
- **Incinerator hall (12 x 10 m)** at the centre: a cut-away industrial building. Inside: the **furnace** (4 x 4 m) against the upper-right wall with a glowing mouth; a **conveyor belt** (2 m wide, 1 m high) running from the lower-left doorway of the hall straight to the furnace mouth; a **control booth** (3 x 3 m, glass on a 1 m base) beside the conveyor. Two 2 m doors: one facing lower-left (conveyor intake), one facing upper-left.
- **Loading dock** along the lower-right side: a raised concrete dock edge (1 m) with two document trucks' parking bays drawn as painted lines only (trucks are sprites), and a 3 m gate in the lower-right wall with a hazard stripe — the main exit.
- **Chimney at TOP corner**: a square brick chimney base (3 x 3 m) with a **steel ladder platform** (2 x 2 m) beside it — a raised sniper perch, reached by a 2 m stair.
- **Gantry crane** over the yard at RIGHT: two crane legs on 1 x 1 m footings with an operator cab drawn on the beam above (the cab is the sniper target).
- **Entry, LEFT corner**: a hole in the perimeter fence (2 m) with a hazard stripe.
- **Bale yard** at BOTTOM: rows of baled paper stacks (2 x 2 m footprints, taller than a person) with 2 m lanes.
- A 3 m service road runs around the hall.

### Do not paint
- No loose objects on the floor: no crates, barrels, chairs, carts, vehicles, boxes, bins, trolleys, plants in pots, signboards. They are added later as separate sprites.
- No characters, people, animals, text, numbers, logos, UI, grid lines, arrows, labels or watermark.

### Before you finish, check
Whole-metre alignment, 1 m wall bands, 2 m doorways, no roofs or upper floors, clean floor, the entry and the second exit both marked with a hazard stripe, and the goal object clearly lit.

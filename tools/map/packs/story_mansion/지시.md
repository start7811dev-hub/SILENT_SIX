## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

Repaint the mansion map `ref/mood_ref.jpg` with the **same mood, materials, lighting and level of detail**, but with the **floor-plan discipline of `ref/look_ref.jpg`**. The previous version cannot be used in the game because its ballroom is octagonal and several rooms stick out of the outline.

Output: one image, exactly **1536x1024**, file name `bg5.jpg`.

### Hard rules
1. **One plain diamond outline**, exactly like `ref/look_ref.jpg`: a single square plan seen in 2:1 isometric, its four outer walls forming one clean diamond that fills the frame the same way. **No wings, no rooms protruding outside the diamond, no notches**, except one small recessed entrance at the bottom corner like the reference.
2. **Every wall runs along one of the two isometric axes.** No diagonal walls, no chamfered corners, no octagon, no curves. The ballroom is a plain **rectangle**.
3. Roof removed, all walls cut at one height (about 46 px), thin, with a flat dark top slab, like the reference.
4. Same scale as the reference: the plan is about 20 x 20 metres; a dining chair is about 12 px wide; doorways and corridors at least 2 metres wide and clearly open.
5. Lay rooms out on a 4-metre rhythm so walls line up.
6. Background outside the diamond: flat near-black `#06080b`.
7. No characters, no people, no text, no UI, no grid lines, no watermark.

### Layout (top = far corner, bottom = entrance corner)
- **Entrance** at the bottom corner: double front doors with a floor hazard stripe outside, opening into a **foyer** with two statues on plinths and a round flower table.
- **Ballroom**: a large rectangle in the centre, entered from the foyer through a wide opening. Three round dinner tables, a grand piano, a chandelier, tall magenta-lit windows painted on its walls. Free floor in the middle.
- A **corridor** wraps round the ballroom on its left, top and right sides, with red runners.
- Left side, off the corridor: a **lounge** with sofas and a fireplace (near the bottom), a **dining room** with a long table (middle), a **service kitchen** (near the top).
- Top corner: a **stair hall** with a red-carpeted staircase going up (decor only).
- Right side, off the corridor: a **library** with armchairs (near the bottom) and, in the upper right, the **private study** with a desk, bookshelves and a large **wall safe** — this is the objective room, as far from the entrance as possible.
- Cover in open areas: banquet tables, sofas, statues on plinths, flower urns, the piano.

Marble and dark wood, gold accents, warm chandeliers and wall sconces, magenta evening light in the windows.

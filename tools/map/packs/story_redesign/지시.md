## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE IMAGE MODEL

You are painting game-map backgrounds for a real-time stealth tactics game. `style/look_ref.jpg` is a finished map from the same game. Every new map must look like it belongs in the same set: **same canvas, same camera, same scale, same wall construction, same level of detail**. Only the place changes.

### Hard rules
1. Output exactly **1536x1024**, landscape. One large diamond-shaped floor plan filling the frame like the reference, 2:1 isometric, same camera angle. Background outside the diamond: flat near-black `#06080b`. No horizon, no sky.
2. **Same scale as the reference.** A single bed in the reference is about 80 px long; a person would be about 45 px tall. Doors, furniture and corridors must be at that scale. The plan is about 20 x 21 metres.
3. **Walls like the reference**: roof removed, every wall cut at the same height (about 50 px), thin, with a flat dark top slab. Walls run only along the two isometric axes. Outdoor maps use fences, containers, rock faces or building blocks the same way: straight, axis-aligned, flat-topped, one common height.
4. **Lay the walls out on a regular rhythm**: room sizes and wall positions in multiples of about 4 metres, like the reference, so corridors and rooms line up. Corridors and doorways at least 2 metres wide and clearly open. No diagonal or curved walls.
5. **Readable floor.** Walkable floor is a calm, slightly brighter tiled or paved surface. Furniture and cover sit clearly on the floor, never blocking a doorway, with free floor around them.
6. Six to nine rooms or zones, at least two different routes from the entrance to the objective room, and several pieces of waist-high cover in the open areas.
7. One clearly marked **entrance** on the lower-left or lower edge (a doorway with a floor hazard stripe, like the reference) and one **objective room** on the far side from it (upper or right part of the plan).
8. **No characters, no people, no animals, no text, no numbers, no UI, no grid lines, no watermark.**
9. Rendering: detailed hand-painted semi-realistic isometric game art, crisp edges, soft baked lighting, a few practical light sources, gentle ambient occlusion. Night.

### Procedure
Work through the maps below in order, **one image per reply**. Before each image write its output file name on its own line. Do not ask questions. After each image wait for "next".
